About

Frank (Shuang) Zhou is an Associate Professor of Accounting (with tenure) at the Tuck School of Business at Dartmouth College. His research studies how information frictions in financial markets interact with managerial decision-making and the economic consequences of these interactions.

He has published his research in leading accounting and finance academic journals and serves on the editorial boards of Journal of Accounting and Economics, The Accounting Review, and Accounting Horizons. He teaches Financial Accounting to MBAs and has received many awards for teaching excellence. Before Tuck, he was an Assistant Professor of Accounting at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania.

Frank holds a PhD in Accounting and an MBA from the University of Chicago, an MSc in Finance from Tilburg University in the Netherlands, and a BS in Finance from Nankai University in China.
